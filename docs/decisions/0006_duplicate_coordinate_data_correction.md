# ADR 0006: Duplicate-Coordinate Data Correction — OTM-Restricted Surface + Paired-Key Masking

## Status

Implemented (2026-06-02) — was Accepted (2026-05-29). Outcome recorded
in the [Outcome](#outcome-2026-06-02--recorded-by-3x14) section below.

## Date

2026-05-29

## Context

A duplicate-coordinate audit on the AV-backed strict surface table
(`data_processed/spy/spy_surface_points_strict.parquet`, 22,512,040
rows) and the `spy_phase1_random40_noiselow` benchmark, executed by
[`scripts/audit_duplicate_coordinates.py`](../../scripts/audit_duplicate_coordinates.py)
on the Pod on 2026-05-29 (2h 39m wall, CPU-only), uncovered a
**structural defect in the modelling assumption** that has been silently
present since Phase 1 and that invalidates the
single-valued-function framing of the IV surface throughout Phase 2 and
Phase 3.

### Root-cause walk-through (committed code paths)

1. The AV `HISTORICAL_OPTIONS` endpoint returns both the call leg and
   the put leg for every listed `(date, expiration, strike)`.
2. [`src/data/03_build_spy_surface_table.py`](../../src/data/03_build_spy_surface_table.py)
   keeps both legs unconditionally — its cleaning gates check only
   per-row finiteness of bid / ask / IV / tau, not contract-level
   uniqueness, and the strict subset (lines 190–203) adds only IV / tau /
   log-moneyness window filters. There is no
   `drop_duplicates(["date","expiration","strike"])`, no OTM convention,
   and no aggregation step.
3. [`src/neural_iv_surface_inference/data/conditional_loaders.py:27`](../../src/neural_iv_surface_inference/data/conditional_loaders.py)
   defines `_CONTEXT_FEATURES = ("log_moneyness", "tau",
   "implied_volatility")` and `_QUERY_FEATURES = ("log_moneyness",
   "tau")`. `type` is **dropped**. The conditional model therefore sees
   two distinct rows at the same model coordinate with two distinct IV
   labels.
4. [`src/neural_iv_surface_inference/models/interpolation.py:45`](../../src/neural_iv_surface_inference/models/interpolation.py)
   fits the RBF on `coords_obs = obs[["log_moneyness", "tau"]].values`.
   The kernel must average over the two legs.
5. [`src/neural_iv_surface_inference/data/masking.py`](../../src/neural_iv_surface_inference/data/masking.py)
   masks rows independently. Whenever one leg is observed and the other
   is held-out, the held-out target sits at L2-distance **exactly zero**
   from an observed point, regardless of true local density.

### Impact on prior results

This does **not** retroactively falsify the Phase 2 closing acceptance
table or the Phase 3 §W11 closing addendum on their own terms — both
sets of numbers were measured under a consistent (if biased) experimental
setup. However:

- The Phase 2 / Phase 3 IV-surface "MAE vs RBF" comparisons are
  **call-put-leakage-biased in RBF's favour** on dense-coordinate
  regions: the RBF interpolation kernel benefits more than the global
  prior from observed exact-coordinate twins of held-out points (37 %
  of held-out rows on test).
- The pending sparse-region ANP-vs-RBF experiment proposed in
  [`docs/research/sparse_region_anp_vs_rbf_design.md`](../research/sparse_region_anp_vs_rbf_design.md)
  is **not interpretable as written**: its Q1 (densest) bucket is
  defined by nearest-observed distance, and ~100 % of zero-distance
  rows are call-put leakage artifacts, not genuine density.
- The label is **not noise**, it is **call-put-quote dispersion**: the
  median 0.049 IV range inside a duplicate group is bigger than the
  Phase 2 `noise_regime: low` σ (0.005) by an order of magnitude. The
  decision-layer reliability claims (coverage within ±2 pp, hi-conf
  MAE strictly below no-abstention) are **likely still valid** because
  both calibration and evaluation see the same dispersion — but this
  must be re-verified on a clean surface before any external write-up.

## Decision

**Adopt the OTM-restricted IV surface as the canonical modelling
substrate, and require paired-coordinate masking as a defence-in-depth
guarantee.**

Two complementary corrections, both required:

### Correction A — Build an OTM-restricted strict surface (Option A from the audit design doc)

Derive a new parquet alongside the existing strict file:

```
data_processed/spy/spy_surface_points_strict_otm.parquet
```

Selection rule, per row:

| Condition | Keep |
|---|---|
| `strike < spot` (`log_moneyness < 0`) | only `type == "put"` |
| `strike > spot` (`log_moneyness > 0`) | only `type == "call"` |
| `strike == spot` (ATM tie) | the side with the **tighter relative spread** `(ask − bid) / max(mid, ε)`; fallback in declared order is `put` if tie remains |

Rationale (industry-standard convention):

- OTM options carry the time-value (volatility-sensitive) part of the
  premium; ITM options are dominated by intrinsic value, where IV is
  more sensitive to the discrete strike grid, dividend approximation
  and early-exercise corrections (SPY is American-style on cash, but
  the standard quote convention still applies).
- This is what CBOE VIX methodology, Bloomberg `OVME`, OptionMetrics
  IvyDB, and every commercial vol-surface vendor uses. See also
  Jackwerth & Rubinstein (1996), Aït-Sahalia & Lo (1998), Bakshi, Cao
  & Chen (1997) on OTM aggregation; the SVI/SSVI literature (Gatheral
  2006; Gatheral & Jacquier 2014) implicitly assumes a single-valued
  surface per slice.
- The result is a true **single-valued function** of `(log_m, tau)` on
  every date, restoring the assumption every downstream model already
  relies on.

Non-goals for Correction A:

- Original strict file and **all existing benchmarks remain untouched**.
  Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3A, Phase 3B artifacts and committed
  evidence stay comparable to themselves and to the historical
  baselines. The OTM surface is a **new** substrate, not a replacement
  of past evidence.
- No aggregation / averaging of call and put IVs (Option B from the
  audit). That would invent a quote no market participant ever
  reported and would destroy the dispersion signal we want to preserve
  as a data-quality diagnostic.

### Correction B — Paired-coordinate masking as a defence-in-depth guarantee

Even after Correction A, two safeguards are required:

1. **Paired-coordinate masking.** Masking must be applied at the level
   of the **rounded `(date, log_m, tau)` key**, not the row. Both rows
   that share a key (if any survive — e.g. legitimate split-strike
   pairs, or future multi-asset extensions) flip observed / held-out
   together. Rationale: this is the only way to *guarantee* zero
   exact-coordinate observed-hidden leakage even if a future data
   source reintroduces structural duplicates. Implementation will live
   in `src/neural_iv_surface_inference/data/masking.py` behind a
   feature flag so the historical benchmarks remain reproducible
   bit-for-bit.
2. **Audit-passes-on-rebuild as a CI invariant.** Re-run
   `scripts/audit_duplicate_coordinates.py` against
   `spy_surface_points_strict_otm.parquet` plus the new OTM benchmark
   and gate the corresponding story on:
   - contract-key dup share ≤ 0.5 %,
   - coord-key dup share ≤ 0.5 % at 10 dp,
   - held-out exact-twin share ≤ 0.5 % per split.

### Rejected alternatives

- **Option B (aggregate call+put into one synthetic surface point).**
  Invents a non-quoted IV; destroys the dispersion signal; breaks
  comparability with the literature's OTM convention. Rejected.
- **Option C (`type` as a model feature).** Re-frames the task from
  *single-valued surface inference* to *raw-quote inference*. Inflates
  apparent accuracy by letting the model memorise the call/put gap,
  and breaks apples-to-apples comparison with the RBF baseline (RBF
  has no `type` channel). Phase 3 framing in
  [ADR 0004](0004_phase3_accuracy_push_framing.md) explicitly defines
  the target as a single-valued surface; reframing the task to escape
  the audit finding would be self-serving. Rejected.
- **Option D only (dedup-aware density, no data rebuild).** A stopgap.
  Cures sparse-region experiment leakage but leaves the training
  target multi-valued. Useful as a sensitivity check on top of
  Correction A, but not a substitute for it.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The IV surface is restored to a single-valued function, the
  assumption every downstream model already relies on.
- The sparse-region ANP-vs-RBF experiment becomes interpretable: Q1
  density is real density, not call-put leakage.
- Phase 3 conclusions can be **re-stated on a clean substrate** before
  any external write-up (interview narrative, paper, talk).
- The OTM convention is the standard practitioner contract, so
  results become directly comparable to vendor data and the SVI / SSVI
  literature — strengthening the interview / paper narrative rather
  than weakening it.

### Negative / costs

- New Phase 3 epic (**3X — Data correction**, inserted between 3B and
  3C in the roadmap) takes a sprint of doc + code + Pod time:
  - data: build the OTM strict file (~1 Pod hour);
  - benchmark: rebuild only `spy_phase1_random40_noiselow` from the OTM
    file (~7 min Pod);
  - re-audit: re-run `scripts/audit_duplicate_coordinates.py` on the
    OTM benchmark (~10 min CPU, with the vectorised v2 of the script
    planned alongside the rebuild);
  - re-fit: re-train ANP point head on the OTM benchmark (~1.5 Pod
    hours; reuses 3B.4 config + the existing checkpoint as warm start
    if structurally compatible);
  - re-eval: re-run 2D.6 decision-layer evaluation (10-date slice +
    full fold) on the clean benchmark (~30 Pod min).
- Some Phase 3B headline numbers will need to be **re-stated** in
  Phase 3D's closing memo on the OTM-clean numbers, with the original
  numbers preserved alongside for traceability. No Phase 3B story is
  retracted; closing addenda are appended.
- Two surface tables now exist in parallel
  (`spy_surface_points_strict.parquet` and
  `spy_surface_points_strict_otm.parquet`). The data lineage doc must
  flag which is canonical for which phase.

### Epic 3X (new) — scope

Three atomic stories, ship in sequence:

1. **3X.1 Decompose Phase 3X.** Write the spec for 3X.2 / 3X.3 (and
   any required 3X.4 re-eval story); update BOARD; commit ADR 0006
   alongside.
2. **3X.2 Remote: build OTM-restricted strict surface + rebuild
   `random40_noiselow` benchmark + re-audit.** Pod-side. Acceptance:
   audit script reports ≤ 0.5 % dup share on all three keys.
3. **3X.3 Remote: re-train ANP point head + re-fit calibrator + re-run
   2D.6 decision-layer eval on the OTM benchmark.** Pod-side.
   Acceptance: matched-pair comparison ANP vs RBF on OTM-clean
   benchmark, with the verdict (still loses by ≥5 %, now wins, or
   somewhere between) committed to the experiment journal.

3C / 3D are **paused** until 3X closes. The sparse-region ANP-vs-RBF
experiment (currently `proposed` in the research note) becomes
runnable only on the OTM benchmark and is moved into 3C scope.

## Open questions deferred to 3X.1

- Whether paired-coordinate masking should be the default
  (`paired_coord=True`) or opt-in for the new OTM benchmark.
- Whether the legacy benchmarks should be **flagged** as "dup-leakage:
  yes" in a small metadata sidecar without rebuilding them, so future
  papers can cite the right baseline.
- Whether to also build an OTM strict file *for the conservative
  (non-strict) dataset* — likely not, as no model trains on it, but
  the audit findings should be documented for the public reader.

The eight decision points (D1–D8) and eight follow-up questions
(Q1–Q8) below are the binding record; the per-story specs implement
them.

| D2 | Retrain the Phase 2D DeepSets family on OTM: single 2D.7-equiv + K=5 2D.8-equiv ensemble (3X.8). |
| D3 | Rebuild **all 11** OTM benchmark variants and audit all of them (3X.4 / 3X.5). |
| D4 | Paired-coordinate masking ships behind a flag, **default off**; the OTM audit gate (3X.5) is the trigger that makes it mandatory if any benchmark shows > 0.5 % exact-twin leakage per split. |
| D5 | Configurable ATM band (default `0.0025`) for the OTM tie-breaker, with sensitivity reporting at `{1e-12, 0.001, 0.0025, 0.005}`; tie-break on tighter relative spread; deterministic documented fallback; **no synthetic-quote construction** (3X.2). If the default band selects an implausible fraction of contracts on real data, stop and write an evidence note for operator approval — do not silently change it. |
| D6 | Keep audit v1 as historical evidence; add vectorised audit v2 with parity tests (3X.3). |
| D7 | Same-type residual duplicates: emit a CSV, classify economic equivalence, drop deterministically if equivalent, else quality-select; `keep='first'` is the documented final fallback with a reported count (3X.2). |

### Training acceptance criteria (revised)

OTM validation NLL ≤ dirty NLL is **not** a pass/fail gate (different
distribution, row count, moneyness coverage, and label-noise
structure). Acceptance instead requires: training completes; no
NaN/inf losses; validation loss decreases from early epochs and
stabilizes; finite valid predictions; prediction row counts match
benchmark query rows; manifests include dataset source hash, config
hash, seed, and code version. Dirty-vs-OTM NLL/MAE comparisons are
reported as **diagnostics**, not gates.

### Primary retraining substrate (operator-confirmed)

The primary dirty-vs-OTM model-family retraining (3X.6–3X.12) runs on
**`spy_phase1_random40_noiselow_otm` only** — the clean apples-to-apples
counterpart to the original dirty `random40_noiselow` experiments. This
isolates the substrate correction (legacy raw call+put benchmark →
clean OTM single-valued benchmark) without simultaneously changing the
masking / noise regime. All 11 OTM variants are still **rebuilt and
audited** (3X.4 / 3X.5) so the corrected benchmark suite is complete and
the deferred robustness study has a substrate to run on.

### Future work — full all-11 OTM retraining (deferred robustness study)

Full GPU-heavy retraining of every model family across all 11 OTM
benchmark variants is **deferred as future work**. It would answer
questions the primary `random40_noiselow_otm` restatement does not:

1. Robustness across masking regimes (does the family ranking hold under
   different observed-context patterns, not just random40?).
2. Robustness across noise regimes (none / low / med / high /
   heteroscedastic).
3. Structured-sparsity behaviour (wing drop, maturity drop, realistic
   liquidity).
4. Architecture-vs-data interaction (does the DeepSets-vs-ANP difference
   depend on the benchmark regime?).
5. Reliability-layer stability (calibration / abstention / confidence /
   decision-layer behaviour across OTM regimes).
6. Support for the later 3C sparse-region / structured-sparsity study on
   a clean substrate, without returning to the dirty benchmark.
7. A stronger generalization claim, if we ever want to assert the
   corrected conclusion is robust across benchmark regimes.

### No-overclaim guardrail (binds 3X.13, 3X.14, and 3D)

- **Correct claim:** *"The original Phase 2D / Phase 3B result was
  restated on the matched clean OTM benchmark `random40_noiselow_otm`."*
- **Forbidden claim:** *"The result is robust across all OTM benchmark
  variants."* — that requires the deferred all-11 retraining study.

## Outcome (2026-06-02) — recorded by 3X.14

The correction was implemented end-to-end and is now the canonical
modelling substrate. Summary of what shipped and what it changed:

- **Build + audit (3X.2–3X.5):** OTM strict surface + 11 OTM benchmarks
  built; audit gate **PASS 12/12** — duplication 93.61 %→**0.0000 %**,
  exact-twin leakage **0.0000 %** across all splits.
- **Restatement (3X.6–3X.13):** full model-family ladder re-run on the
  matched `spy_phase1_random40_noiselow_otm`. Every family improves
  3–11× on clean data; RBF floor 0.0662→**0.00613** (10.8×).
- **Q5 architecture check — no reversal.** ANP still beats DeepSets at
  matched head on OTM (point 1.77×, ensemble 1.31×, quantile 1.21×,
  gaussian 1.06×). The DeepSets→ANP cross-attention thesis survives, so
  **no Phase 2 reopen and no architecture-revising retrospective/ADR
  addendum** are warranted (the Q5 trigger did not fire).
- **RBF-vs-ANP verdict — unchanged in direction, wider in magnitude.**
  ANP's gap to RBF grew from the dirty +2.7 % (best point head) to
  **+61 %** on clean test MAE (calibrated production predictor +90 %).
  The dirty call-put confound had been flattering ANP by penalizing
  RBF's interpolation of two-valued targets. Phase 3 bar still NOT MET.
- **Scope:** matched substrate only; all-11 robustness retraining
  deferred (no-overclaim guardrail honored).
