## Purpose

Phase-entry decomposition story for epic 2E (**Phase 2 Follow-ups**). It opens
the follow-up backlog that was implicitly created when Phase 2 closed on
2026-05-25 — diagnostics and small-scope sweeps that test assumptions baked
into the production `ConditionalSurfaceModel` and decision layer but do not
belong in Phase 3 scoping.

Mandatory first story of every epic per the operating model §4
("phase-entry decomposition"), mirroring the precedent of
[`2D.1_decompose_phase_2d.md`](2D.1_decompose_phase_2d.md).

Unlike epic 2D, epic 2E is **open-ended**: stories are added by progressive
decomposition as follow-ups are identified, not enumerated up front. 2E.1
opens the epic and registers the first follow-up story (2E.2 — latent
capacity diagnostic) prompted by the operator's question "how many of our
latent dimensions are actively contributing?". Further 2E stories will be
added by future decomposition passes.

## Story breakdown (the decomposition)

Initial breakdown — **one** local-plus-remote story under workstream W6
(capacity & representation diagnostics). Additional stories will be added
as follow-ups surface.

| Story | Scope | Workstream | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2E.2 Latent capacity diagnostic | Effective rank + PCA on the W3 `SetEncoder` latent `z_t` extracted from the production 2D.7 gaussian checkpoint, plus a `latent_dim ∈ {8, 16, 32, 64, 96, 128}` sweep retrained on the AV benchmark. New `diagnostics/latent_probe.py` + `diagnostics/effective_rank.py` + two runner scripts + sweep config. Phase A (analysis on existing checkpoint) and Phase B (sweep) both run remote because `data_processed/spy/benchmarks/` is empty locally. | W6 | 2D.7 |

### Phase sequencing (initial)

1. Human reviews and promotes 2E.2 to `todo`.
2. A future session implements 2E.2 per its spec. Diagnostic-module code
   and unit tests are written and verified locally on synthetic tensors;
   the actual extraction on the production checkpoint and the latent-dim
   sweep both execute on RunPod.
3. Findings are appended to `docs/experiments/experiment_journal.md` and
   to `docs/phase2_result_memo.md` as a follow-up addendum.
