## Purpose

Convert the two 3A.3 artifact bundles into the **single piece of
evidence** that 3A exists to produce: a side-by-side **Fourier vs
raw** evaluation against the same Phase 2D.9 baselines (RBF
interpolation + 2D.7 Gaussian), with a one-paragraph addendum to the
Phase 3 roadmap recording the measured delta. That delta is the
*input* to 3B's coordinate-encoding default decision — it does not
gate 3B (3A and 3B are parallel by ADR 0004), but it *does* close
epic 3A.

Local-only. Read-only on the 3A.3 artifacts. No training, no Pod time.

## Scope contract (atomic story)

- **One question:** what is the val/test Gaussian NLL, test MAE,
  coverage (calibrated 0.90 target), and high-confidence MAE delta
  of Fourier-encoded `(k, τ)` vs raw `(k, τ)` on the
  `spy_phase1_random40_noiselow` AV benchmark, against the same
  2D.9 baselines?
- **One artifact bundle:**
  `results/3/spy_phase1_random40_noiselow/{3a_fourier,3a_raw}/` —
  each with `metrics_summary.csv` (one row), `calibration_table.csv`,
  `abstention_curve.csv`, and a paired side-by-side `comparison.csv`
  at `results/3/spy_phase1_random40_noiselow/3a_compare/`.
- **One acceptance check:** the roadmap addendum names the measured
  Fourier-vs-raw delta (point estimate, plus whether it closes a
  measurable fraction of the gap to RBF) with file references back
  to the committed metrics CSVs.

## Dependencies
