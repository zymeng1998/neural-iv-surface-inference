## Purpose

Phase-entry decomposition story for epic **3C — Feature &
Inductive-Bias Expansion** under workstream **W12**. Two orthogonal
directions; 3C.1 decides whether to ship one or both, based on the
results of 3A and 3B:

1. **Microstructure features in `O_t`.** Expand the per-quote tuple
   from `(log_moneyness, τ, iv_input)` to include `bid`, `ask`,
   `bid_ask_spread`, `mid_price`, `volume`, `open_interest`,
   `put_call_indicator`, and exogenous state (`spot_level`, `vix`,
   `recent_realized_vol_5d`, `days_to_next_earnings_or_dividend`).
   Locks the feature list in **ADR 0006**.
2. **SVI / SSVI parameterized head.** Output `θ_t ∈ R^5` (SVI per
   slice) and evaluate the SVI formula at `(k, τ)`. Built-in
   no-arbitrage structure under SSVI. Locks the parameterization in
   **ADR 0007** if shipped.

3C.1 picks scope (microstructure only / SVI head only / both) based
on:

- 3A and 3B's measured MAE gap to the Phase 3 acceptance bar.
- The forbidden-flag violation count from 3B's decision-layer eval
  (high counts → SVI head's structural inductive bias is more
  valuable).
