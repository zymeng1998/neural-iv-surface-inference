## Purpose

Phase-entry decomposition story for epic 2B (Phase 2 Workstream W2 — Masking
Sensitivity and Structure Diagnostics). It pulls epic 2B into active work, breaks
it into atomic stories each sized for one focused session, writes a spec per
story, and registers them on the board at `backlog`. Mandatory first story of
every epic per the operating model §4 ("phase-entry decomposition").

W2 probes reliability through **perturbation** (re-masking stability) and
**structural validity** (no-arbitrage diagnostics), independent of any single
model — and feeds `no_arb_risk_flags` into the W5 decision layer. Diagnostics
come *before* hard structural constraints: we quantify violations before deciding
whether to penalize them inside a model objective.
