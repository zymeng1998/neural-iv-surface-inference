## Purpose

The **decision-bearing** story. From the cached 4B.4 hybrid predictions and the
4B.3 per-rung RBF, compute — per regime × rung — RBF MAE, hybrid σ̂ MAE, the
absolute and relative delta, and a **date-clustered paired-bootstrap 95 % CI**
on the per-query delta (same methodology as 4A.7). Assemble the **edge-vs-
sparsity trajectory** (the relative edge as a function of rung, per regime) and
**adjudicate the ADR 0011 gate**: does the hybrid's edge over RBF grow
materially as the context thins, or stay in the ~0–2 % band?

## Where this runs

**Local (Mac), CPU-only.** From cached CSVs — no pod, no checkpoint (mirrors
4A.7).

## Dependencies

- 4B.4 `done` (per-rung σ̂ predictions cached local).
- 3X.6 / 4A baseline (the dense-end RBF floor + hybrid reference).

## Scope contract (atomic story)

- **One question:** how does the hybrid's relative edge over RBF behave across
  the sparsity ladder, and which side of the ADR 0011 gate does it land?
- **One artifact bundle:** `results/4/…/4b_sweep/` — `trajectory.csv`,
  `trajectory_wide.md`, per-regime CI tables, `gate_verdict.json`.
- **One acceptance check:** every regime × rung has a delta + date-clustered CI;
  `gate_verdict.json` records `accuracy_survives ∈ {true, false, ambiguous}`
  against a pre-registered rule.

## Gate rule (pre-registered, from ADR 0011 / roadmap)

- **Survives (`true`):** the relative edge **increases across the ladder** in at
  least the wing-sensitive regimes, and the sparse-end date-clustered CI on the
  delta is entirely < 0 with magnitude materially above the dense-end ~2 %
  (e.g. ≥ ~5 % relative at the sparse end). → accuracy stays a core story.
- **Retired (`false`):** the relative edge stays within ~0–2 % across all rungs
  (no material growth). → stop accuracy chasing; Phase 5 reliability-first
  becomes primary.
- **Ambiguous:** trend present but CIs do not separate, or the eval-time
  fairness caveat clouds the read → triggers the conditional retrain
  escalation 4B.7. The exact numeric thresholds are frozen in this story before
  looking at the trajectory.
