## Purpose

Assemble the deliverable that does not exist anywhere today: a
**matched dirty-vs-OTM comparison** across the full baseline ladder on
`random40_noiselow`. Every legacy dirty number is **cited, not re-run**
(from the committed 3A/3B/2D bundles); every OTM number comes from
3X.6–3X.12. A `data_substrate ∈ {dirty, otm}` column makes the pairing
explicit.

## Where this runs

**Local Mac, CPU** — pure table assembly over committed CSV bundles.

## Scope contract (atomic story)

- **One question:** for each (model family × head × metric × split), what
  is the dirty value, the OTM value, and the delta — across RBF, MLP,
  DeepSets (single + ensemble), ANP (3 heads + ensemble), and the
  calibrated/decision-layer view?
- **One artifact bundle:** `results/3/spy_phase1_random40_noiselow_otm/
  3x_compare/comparison.csv` (long-format: family, head, data_substrate,
  split, metric, value, source, n) + a wide human-readable
  `comparison_wide.csv`/`.md`.
- **One acceptance check:** every cell traces to a committed source
  bundle (no orphan numbers); no NaN; dirty and OTM both populated for
  every family present on both substrates.
