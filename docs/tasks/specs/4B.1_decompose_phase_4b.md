   trajectory rather than duplicating the chain ×4.

   trajectory is computed (default: relative edge increases across rungs in the
   wing-sensitive regimes *and* the sparse-end date-clustered CI clears the
   dense-end ~2 % margin; ≥ ~5 % relative at the sparse end ⇒ survives).
