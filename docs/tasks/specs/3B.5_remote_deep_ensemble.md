## Purpose

Remote counterpart to 2D.8, applied to the ANP architecture. Train K
independent seeded copies (default K = 5) of the ANP cross-attention
conditional model on the AV benchmark, score them through the existing
`EnsembleConditionalPredictor` adapter (which, by 3B.3, already
handles either decoder kind), and emit the per-row ensemble val/test
predictions plus the disagreement std that 3B.6 (calibration) and
3B.7 (decision-layer scoring) consume.

The architecture, training loop, and ensembling adapter are unchanged
from 2D.3 / 2D.8 — pure replication across K seeds of the ANP
architecture trained in 3B.4.

## Where this runs

**Remote (RunPod) only.** K full ANP trainings on the AV benchmark.
Member checkpoints live Pod-side under
`runs/3B5/seed_{seed}/checkpoints/`; the manifest + ensemble-
aggregated val/test predictions + per-member per-epoch curves are
pulled back to the laptop.

## Scope contract (atomic story)

- **One question:** what are the ensemble-mean test MAE and per-row
  disagreement-std distribution of a K=5 deep ensemble of the ANP
  architecture on the AV benchmark, versus the single-seed 3B.4
  point baseline?
- **One artifact bundle:** `artifacts/runs/3B5/` with `manifest.json`
  (members + per-member final metrics), `members.json` (K rows),
  `training_curves.csv` (per-member per-epoch), and
  `val_predictions.csv` / `test_predictions.csv` (ensemble mean +
  disagreement std + per-member columns), schema-matched to 2D.8.
- **One acceptance check:** all K members complete; `members.json`
  lists exactly K rows with the configured seeds;
  `disagreement_std` is finite, non-negative, and non-degenerate.

## Non-goals

- Do NOT change the ANP architecture or training loop — pure
  replication across seeds.
- Do NOT use the gaussian or quantile head here. The ensemble
  produces a *disagreement* signal; the per-element predictive
  variance comes from 3B.4's gaussian / quantile heads. They are
  combined in 3B.6.
- Do NOT calibrate disagreement into probability — **3B.6**.
- Do NOT pull back member checkpoints (size). Manifest + aggregated
  CSVs only, matching the 2D.8 convention.
- Do NOT modify the AV dataset.
- Do NOT modify `EnsembleConditionalPredictor` — any issue is a 3B.3
  bug; fix there.

## Compute requirements

This story has two compute phases.

| Phase | Locale | Hardware | VRAM | Wall time | Disk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (1) Config + launcher | local Mac | CPU-only | n/a | ~15 min dev | <50 KB |
| (2) K = 5 full ANP point-head trainings + ensemble scoring | remote (RunPod) | single GPU — same tier as 3B.4 | ~8–12 GB | ~45–60 min wall per member × 5 sequential = **~4–5 h total Pod wall**, plus ~10 min ensemble scoring | ~300 MB checkpoints per member × 5 ≈ 1.5 GB (gitignored, Pod-side); ~50 MB committed (manifest + members.json + curves + aggregated predictions) |

- **Disk — Pod working set:** ~1.5 GB checkpoints + ~500 MB
  intermediate tensors + ~100 MB logs → **allocate ≥ 8 GB free on
  the Pod volume**. Reuse the same Pod / volume as 3B.4 so the AV
  benchmark is already on disk; exclude it from this number.
- **Recommended Pod rental window:** ~6 h to cover sequential
  member training + ensemble scoring + rsync + buffer.
- **Sequential vs parallel:** the 5 members are independent. If the
  operator wants ~5× less wall, they can run on 5 Pods concurrently;
  the cost is the same total GPU-hours. Default is sequential on a
  single Pod (simpler bookkeeping, single log stream).
- **Notes:** ensemble training is the longest remote phase in epic
  3B. Plan rental immediately *after* 3B.4 completes so the Pod
  stays warm with the configs + venv intact; do not spin up a fresh
  Pod for 3B.5 if a 3B.4 Pod is still alive.
