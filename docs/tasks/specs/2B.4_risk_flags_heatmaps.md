## Purpose

Synthesize the two W2 signals into decision-ready outputs: derive
`no_arb_risk_flags` from the no-arbitrage diagnostics (2B.3) — optionally
modulated by masking instability (2B.2) — and visualize both as
`(log_moneyness, tau)`-binned region heatmaps. This produces the per-point /
per-region risk surface that the W5 decision layer (2D) consumes, and the
"uncertainty heatmaps over (k, tau) regions" the roadmap calls for.

## Non-goals

- Do NOT choose tradability thresholds or an `abstain_flag` policy — that is W5
  (2D). Flags here are diagnostic (boolean/severity), not trading decisions.
- Do NOT re-implement the diagnostic checks (2B.3) or the masking harness (2B.2)
  — consume their outputs.
- Do NOT build the end-to-end runner or write artifacts to disk — that is 2B.5.

## Implementation steps

1. `risk_flags.py`: `derive_risk_flags(diagnostics, instability=None, config) ->`
   per-point boolean `no_arb_risk_flag` + continuous `risk_score`. Document the
   combination rule (e.g. flag if any structural violation touches the point, or
   severity/instability exceeds a configurable threshold). Pure, typed.
2. `bin_to_regions(values, log_moneyness, tau, ...) -> 2D grid` reusing the
   `TAU_BUCKETS` / `MONEYNESS_BUCKETS` definitions so region grids align with
   the W1 joint-heatmap convention.
3. `viz/diagnostic_plots.py`: `plot_risk_region_heatmap` and
   `plot_instability_heatmap` returning Matplotlib Figures (caller owns saving),
   reusing the existing heatmap styling.
4. Unit-test: a surface with a known violation region produces flags only there;
   region binning matches the eval bucket edges; heatmap functions return Figures
   on synthetic data; threshold config behaves monotonically.
