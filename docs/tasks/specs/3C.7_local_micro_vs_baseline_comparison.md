## Purpose

Build side-by-side comparison tables on the matched
`spy_phase1_random40_noiselow_otm` substrate between

- **OTM baseline (no microstructure):** RBF-on-OTM (3X.6), ANP three
  heads (3X.9), ANP K=5 ensemble (3X.10), calibrated production
  predictor (3X.12).
- **OTM + `micro_v1`:** ANP three heads (3C.3), ANP K=5 ensemble
  (3C.4), calibrated production predictor (3C.6).

The story produces both a long-format CSV (one row per
family × head × variant) and a wide-format CSV (paired columns
`{baseline, micro_v1}` per metric), plus a per-family delta column.
Every number cited from a committed bundle (no re-running).

This is the analog of 3X.13. The 3C closing addendum (3C.8) consumes
these tables.

## Where this runs

**Local (Mac), CPU-only.** Reads only already-committed metrics CSVs.

- **One question:** at family × head granularity, what is the

  `spy_phase1_random40_noiselow_otm`, and what is the ANP+`micro_v1`
  -vs-RBF-on-OTM relative gap on the calibrated production predictor?

  `results/3/spy_phase1_random40_noiselow_otm/3c_compare/` containing
