## Purpose

Retrain the Phase 3B **ANP cross-attention** conditional model on
`random40_noiselow_otm` across **all three heads** — gaussian,
quantile, point_control (D1) — a faithful head-by-head restatement of
3B.4 on the clean substrate. This is the headline model of the
dirty-vs-OTM comparison.

## Where this runs

**Remote (RunPod), GPU pod.** Sequential trainings mirroring 3B.4.
Checkpoints Pod-side; manifests + curves + per-row predictions pulled
back.

## Scope contract (atomic story)

- **One question:** what are the ANP-on-OTM val/test NLL, test MAE, and
  quantile coverage/monotonicity for all three heads on
  `random40_noiselow_otm`, vs the dirty 3B.4 numbers (test MAE 0.0726
  gaussian / 0.0681 quantile / 0.0684 point)?
- **One artifact bundle:** `artifacts/runs/3X9/{gaussian,quantile,
  point_control}/` mirroring the 3B.4 bundle schema.
- **One acceptance check:** all three complete, finite losses, quantile
  monotonic on test, schemas match 3B.4, manifests carry
  dataset/config/seed/code hashes; dirty-vs-OTM deltas journaled.
