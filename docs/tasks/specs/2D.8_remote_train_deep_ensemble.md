## Purpose

Remote counterpart to **story 2D.3**. Train K independent seeded copies
(default K = 5) of the W3 conditional model on the Alpha Vantage benchmark,
score them through the `EnsembleConditionalPredictor` adapter landed
locally in 2D.3, and emit the per-row ensemble val/test predictions plus
the disagreement std that 2D.4 (calibration) and 2D.9 (end-to-end run)
consume.

The architecture and training loop are unchanged from 2C — pure replication
across K seeds with the `members.json` manifest format defined in 2D.3.

## Where this runs

**Remote (RunPod) only.** GPU-heavy: K full conditional training runs on the
AV benchmark. Member checkpoints live Pod-side under
`checkpoints/conditional/ensemble/seed_{seed}/`; only the manifest and
ensemble-aggregated val/test predictions are pulled back.

## Non-goals

- Do NOT change the architecture or training loop — pure replication.
- Do NOT introduce a heteroscedastic / quantile head — that is **2D.7**.
- Do NOT calibrate disagreement into probability — **2D.4**.
- Do NOT pull back member checkpoints (size). Manifest + aggregated CSVs
  only.
- Do NOT modify the AV dataset.

## Implementation steps

1. Materialize `configs/conditional_2D8_ensemble.yaml` from
   `configs/conditional.yaml` with `ensemble.size: 5`,
   `ensemble.seeds: [101, 202, 303, 404, 505]`, and `head.kind: point`
   (ensemble disagreement is a separate uncertainty source from the 2D.7
   head — they are combined in 2D.4).
2. On the Pod, run `scripts/run_ensemble_train.py --config
   configs/conditional_2D8_ensemble.yaml` to produce the K seeded
   checkpoints + `members.json`.
3. Score the ensemble on val + test via the
   `EnsembleConditionalPredictor` adapter and write:
   - per-row `val_predictions.csv` / `test_predictions.csv` with columns
     `sigma_hat, disagreement_std, member_0..member_{K-1}`,
   - per-member per-epoch `training_curves.csv`,
   - a top-level `manifest.json` carrying config hash, git SHA, dataset
     version, K, seeds, per-member final val/test MAE, wall-clock.
4. Rsync the manifest + CSVs (not checkpoints) to
   `artifacts/runs/2D8/`.
5. Append an `experiment_journal.md` entry: per-member final metrics,
   ensemble-mean test MAE, mean disagreement, comparison to the 2C point
   single-seed baseline.
6. Update `docs/logs/progress_log.md`.
7. Run the live PMR gate.
