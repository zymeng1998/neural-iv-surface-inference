## Purpose

Retrain the K=5 ANP point-head deep ensemble on `random40_noiselow_otm`,
mirroring 3B.5 on the clean substrate. Supplies the ensemble
disagreement signal that the OTM calibrator (3X.11) fuses and the OTM
decision-layer eval (3X.12) consumes.

## Where this runs

**Remote (RunPod), GPU pod.** Five sequential point-head trainings,
seeds `[101, 202, 303, 404, 505]`. Checkpoints Pod-side; aggregated
predictions + members manifest pulled back.

## Scope contract (atomic story)

- **One question:** what is the K=5 ANP ensemble-mean test MAE and the
  per-row disagreement σ on `random40_noiselow_otm`, vs the dirty 3B.5
  ensemble (disagreement mean 0.0121)?
- **One artifact bundle:** `artifacts/runs/3X10/{members.json,
  member_*/, aggregated_predictions_{val,test}.parquet}`.
- **One acceptance check:** five members complete, finite losses,
  aggregated predictions written, disagreement σ reported; manifests
  carry per-member dataset/config/seed/code hashes.

## Compute requirements

- **Locale:** remote (RunPod)
- **Hardware class:** single GPU (3B.5 tier)
- **Approx. VRAM:** ~8–12 GB
- **Approx. wall time:** ~45–60 min × 5 members → **~4–5 h total**
- **Disk — local committed artifacts:** members.json + aggregated
  predictions (~30 MB; gitignore large parquets)
- **Disk — remote working set:** 5×~300 MB checkpoints → **≥ 6 GB free

  the two heaviest items; both are reliability-layer completeness, not
  the core architecture anchor.

## Implementation steps

1. Confirm 3X.5 gate PASSED and 3X.9 done.
2. Clone the 3B.5 ensemble config; repoint dataset to
   `random40_noiselow_otm`; keep seeds.
3. Train 5 members sequentially, each logged.
4. Aggregate predictions; compute disagreement σ.
5. Pull back members.json + aggregated predictions; journal dirty-vs-OTM
   disagreement contrast.
6. PMR gate dry-run; flip BOARD to `in_review`.
